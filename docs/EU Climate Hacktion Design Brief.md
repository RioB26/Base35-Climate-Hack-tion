Design brief: logo and brand for our landfill methane tool
What it is
A free web tool for Australia and New Zealand that helps councils, landfill owners and policy-makers decide which landfills to fix first to cut methane. It works in four steps:

Find: a globe with landfills pinned, plus a search box.
Check: compares what a site reports with what the Sentinel-5P satellite sees downwind.
Fix: a short questionnaire (capture target, start year, power price, costs) next to a 3D model of the landfill.
Fund: shows the budget needed, the electricity and money the project makes, a COP31 alignment score, and a ranking of every site by cost per tonne of CO₂-e avoided.
Built for Climate Hack-tion 2026 (zero waste and methane, "Build for 2035"). The tone is honest and calm: the satellite gives a screening signal, not proof.

Name
The working name is "Methane Payback", and we want a new one. Please show logo ideas for Tipwatch first. "Tip" is what Australians and New Zealanders call the dump, and "watch" stands for the satellite. Alternatives: Wasteward, Seepwatch, Plumewise, Tip to Power.

Design decisions already made (please keep)

Soft tints: #dfe7df, #e3eefb, #fbe9df. The methane ramp runs from #fbe9df to #a4512a.

Type.

Fraunces serif for headings and big numbers, with italic words in tangerine for emphasis.
Inter for body and UI text.
Small uppercase, letter-spaced labels in tangerine.
Shapes. Rounded cards (18px), pill buttons, thin warm borders, soft shadows and plenty of space. The feel is modern but natural, close to Claude's own design style.

Visuals.

A globe with a pale blue ocean (#dbe6ee) and sand-coloured land (#eeebe1).
Tangerine map pins with a white ring.
Square satellite pixels for methane.
A low-poly 3D landfill: sage-green terraces, cone trees, tangerine gas wells and an evergreen engine shed.
The current placeholder mark is a rounded leaf/pin shape split diagonally into evergreen and tangerine.
Logo concepts to explore
Mound and orbit: a stepped three-terrace mound (like our 3D model) with a thin sky-blue orbit arc over it and a small satellite dot.
Plume to leaf: a tangerine methane wisp rising from the mound that curls into an evergreen leaf, so captured gas turns into something good.
Pin and rings: a map pin whose head holds two thin target rings (our 10 and 30 km satellite search rings) over a small mound.
Avoid smokestacks, recycling arrows, skulls or warning signs.
